Member privileges

More than discounts.

The strongest reason to become a member should never be a percentage off a bill. It should be access, recognition and experiences that are difficult to reproduce elsewhere.

01

Priority Access

Members receive earlier access to rooms, experiences, events and limited-capacity activities.

02

Remembered

Preferences, routines and details can be recorded so returning guests don't have to explain themselves again.

03

Private Experiences

Certain experiences would only be available to members and invited guests.

04

Your People

Membership includes controlled guest access, allowing the experience to be shared without turning VARENA into an open-access club.

The difference

You are remembered.

The most valuable part of membership is not a wristband, card or logo. It is arriving and realizing that the people around you already know how you like your coffee, which room you prefer and whether you want conversation or silence.

This is the service philosophy behind the membership concept — human recognition first, technology second.
